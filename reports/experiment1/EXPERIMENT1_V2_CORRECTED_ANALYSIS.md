# Experiment 1 — v2 Corrected Analysis (no re-run)

This re-computes the metrics and statistics the independent review flagged,
directly from the archived 960-run logs (`archive/experiment1_v1/runs/`). No
simulation was re-run. Generated by `tools/analyze_experiment1_v2.py`.

## What changed

1. **air_intervention** now derives from the *issued* execution
   (`critical_mission_final_mode == "AIR"`), not from proposed manager actions.
   This removes the 40 checker-rejected DISPATCH-to-busy proposals that v1
   mistakenly counted as air intervention.
2. **signed seconds saved** vs B0 is reported (negative = slower than ground),
   separately from the truncated DE proxy.
3. **unnecessary air** is re-based on the corrected air intervention (frozen
   "ground could meet deadline" predicate unchanged).
4. **statistics** now include Wilcoxon signed-rank, Holm correction over the
   comparison family, t-quantile 95 % CI (n−1 dof), a direct B4-vs-B2 contrast,
   and exact deterministic deltas (effect size marked *undefined*) instead of a
   spurious p = 0.

## What these corrected numbers can and cannot say

- **Can say (as a v1 descriptive result with model boundaries):** on this frozen
  deterministic prototype, B2's completion/violation/damage profile is better
  than B4's, and B4 over-intervenes relative to B2.
- **Cannot say:** that the LLM's selective-scheduling ability is *inherently*
  inferior — the v1 prompt under-specified the preemption action, the v1 Global
  State withheld the physical ETA/cost features B2 received, and the LLM calls
  were not confirmed independent. Those are fixed in the v2 design before any
  attribution is claimed.
