## 0.3 Old Experiment-1 primary dataset — superseded

The previous Experiment-1 primary dataset is **`runs/experiment1_v2/`** (276 runs:
12 scenarios × {B0, B1, B2} + 12 × {B4v2a, B4v2b} × 10 same-prompt repeats) and the
archived **`archive/experiment1_v1/runs/`** (960 deterministic runs).

**`superseded_for_primary_analysis = true`** for both. They are retained ONLY for
provenance / debugging / backend-cache diagnostic, and **must not be mixed with the
corrected primary dataset in any statistic.**

The corrected primary dataset is `runs/experiment1_final/` (independent-seed,
paired; see `tools/run_experiment1_final.py`).

### Reasons the old data is not the final statistical primary

1. **normalize-then-validate** was not applied to the old primary path (v1 validated
   raw actions before route/target completion).
2. **environment-computed ground ETA** was not the completion basis in v1.
3. **seed fix** — the old runs reused a single frozen `scenario_seed` (SUMO/BlueSky
   deterministic), so no independent simulation seed existed.
4. **cache/independence capture** — same-prompt ×10 repeats hit the backend cache
   (~80 % sub-second cached completions), so n=10 was not independent.
5. **ISSUED-based metrics** — v1 counted checker-rejected proposals as interventions.
6. **Action Contract v2** — `prompts/manager_v2.txt` states the REASSIGN preemption
   contract that v1 omitted.
7. **candidate information redesign** — `orchestrator/candidate_info.py` v2.0.0 now
   exposes the full manager-agnostic candidate facts (ETA, completion time,
   predicted deadline violation, preemption/service-loss, destination availability,
   landing compatibility, battery/endurance) to every manager.
